Frequently asked questions

How do I place an order?

Open any product page and press “Order on WhatsApp”, or message us on our Facebook page. We confirm availability, price and delivery with you in the chat.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We'll share the available payment options with you when you message us to order.

Can I see more photos before buying?

Yes. Message us and we'll send extra photos or a short video of the bag you like.

Do the bags fit a laptop?

Each product page lists the laptop size it fits under “Product details”.

Shipping & delivery

We deliver to your address. Delivery time and cost depend on your location, and we confirm both with you before you pay. You'll get an update when your order is on its way.

Returns & exchanges

If your bag arrives damaged or isn't what you ordered, contact us within 7 days of delivery with a photo and we'll arrange a replacement or refund. Items should be unused and in original condition.

Bag care

  • Wipe clean with a soft, damp cloth.
  • Avoid soaking the bag or machine washing it.
  • Air dry away from direct heat or sunlight.
  • Don't overload beyond the stated capacity.

Contact Us

Questions about a bag or ready to order? Message us directly and we'll reply as soon as we can.
